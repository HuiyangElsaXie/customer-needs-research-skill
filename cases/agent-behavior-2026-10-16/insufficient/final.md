## Neutral interview questions
1. Tell me about the most recent time you arranged an event involving other people. What were you trying to accomplish?
2. Walk me through what you did and what tools or people were involved.
3. What went smoothly, and where, if anywhere, did the process become difficult?
4. What happened as a result, and what did you do next? How typical was this compared with other recent events?
5. How did you choose the approach you used, and who else influenced or approved that choice?
